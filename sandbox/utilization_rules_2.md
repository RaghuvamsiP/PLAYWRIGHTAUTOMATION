Simple Audit Calculations
How the credit audit generated by POST /api/report (with simpleAudit=1) calculates each figure, which credit report fields it reads, and a worked example using a real QA client, with every number traced back to its accounts.

code: import_log_changes_prod
example: company 1003532 · client 4 (QA)
provider: SmartCredit
01
How the audit is built
Import. POST /api/report with simpleAudit=1 fetches the report and saves it as a pending report (generateSimpleAudit=0), or re-uses the last stored report (generateSimpleAudit=1).
Pre-calculation during import. The provider's Simple Audit transformer reads the normalised report and stores totals under simple_audit_data: credit limits, balances, negative accounts, public records and inquiries, per bureau. SmartCredit and legacy MyFreeScoreNow use Transformers/SmartCredit/SimpleAuditTransformer; every other provider uses Transformers/SimpleAuditTransformer.
Calculation. SimpleAuditReportService::processSimpleAuditReport() turns those totals into the figures shown (averages, usage percentage, positive and negative counts) and reads the score and summary counts from credit_score and credit_summary.
Template. The company's audit template (crd_audit_template) is filled by TemplateService::renderTemplate(), which replaces placeholders such as {TOTAL REVOLVING CREDIT LINES}. A headless browser draws the score gauges.
Save. The HTML is stored in crd_clients_simple_audit, one audit per report (re-generating overwrites it).
Bureau order on the audit's score cards is Equifax, TransUnion, Experian.

Which accounts count towards limits and balances
SmartCredit rule: the account must be Open, have a Credit Limit above 0, and be a Credit Card (TransUnion also accepts the "revolving" types). The balance is added only when the limit is counted.

Total available revolving credit = (1,000 + 1,900 + 1,900) ÷ 3 = 1,600. It is the average of the three bureau totals, not a sum.
Current credit card balance = (1,045 + 1,973 + 2,049) ÷ 3 = 1,689. Divided by the number of bureaus that have counted cards, here 3.
Credit Card Usage = average of each bureau's own rounded percentage = (105 + 104 + 108) ÷ 3 = 105.7, rounded to 106%. 75% or more is rated Very Poor.

Account rules by provider
Rule	SmartCredit, MyFreeScoreNow (legacy)	IdentityIQ, MyScoreIQ, PrivacyGuard, Sample, MyFreeScoreNow (new)
Account Status	Open	Open (exact)
Account Type counted	Equifax and Experian: credit card only. TransUnion: credit card or a revolving type	revolving, revolving or option, revolving charge account, revolving account, credit card - revolving terms. "Credit Card" is not counted.
Limit field	Credit Limit, only if > 0	Limit, else Credit Limit; a $0 limit still counts the bureau
Balance field	Balance Owed, only if > 0	Balance, else Balance Owed
Cents	dropped ($1,234.99 → 1,234)
Negative account	Account Rating "Derogatory", or any Late Payment Status, or a negative keyword in Payment Status / Creditor Remarks	any Late Payment Status, or a negative keyword in Account Type, Late Payment Status or Comments (MyScoreIQ also Payment Status)
Collections card	always 0 (no key)	from credit_summary where present; PrivacyGuard from its collections section
Inquiries / public records cards	from credit_summary	PrivacyGuard and Sample: always 0
CreditHeroScore audits use figures supplied in the CHS payload (totalAvailableRevolvingCredit, currentCreditCardBalance, credit_utilization) when present, instead of these calculations.

05
Things to know
Behaviour	Effect
"Total available revolving credit" is divided by 3 always	It's an average, not a total. If only TransUnion has cards with $9,000, the audit shows $3,000.
Limit ÷ 3 but balance ÷ n	When a bureau has no cards, the limit and balance use different divisors, so balance ÷ limit doesn't match the usage %.
Usage is an average of per-bureau rounded percentages	Not total balance ÷ total limit. It can exceed 100% (client 4: 106%) and make "percentage left" negative.
Generic providers skip "Credit Card" type	IdentityIQ, MyScoreIQ, PrivacyGuard and new-MyFreeScoreNow cards labelled "Credit Card" are left out of limits and balances.
SmartCredit Equifax/Experian accept only "credit card"	Cards typed "Revolving" on those bureaus are left out.
Derogatory items and inquiries count bureau hits	One account negative on 3 bureaus counts 3 (client 4: 5 items for 3 accounts).
Negative count needs a valid Date Opened	A negative account with a missing or invalid date is listed but not counted on that bureau's card.
Positive vs Negative	Negative includes public records; Positive subtracts only negative accounts.
No-data bureau shows 0%	Its pie shows "excellent" instead of "No Data".
Company address, phone and fax placeholders	Always blank: the template reads different column names than the query returns.
generateSimpleAudit sent as text "0"	The audit is silently skipped; it must be the number 0.
Formulas read from SimpleAuditReportService, TemplateService and the two SimpleAuditTransformer classes on import_log_changes_prod. The worked example uses the stored report and audit for company 1003532, client 4 on the QA database (read only); every value was recalculated and matches the saved audit.
